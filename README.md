# Author's Seat Simulator

An interactive simulation created for **LTPS 240 (Learning Technology and Performance Systems) at Texas A&M University**, as part of our team's draft training module, *UDL-Informed Peer Review*, for reviewers of the *International Journal of Universal Design and Universal Design for Learning* (IJUDUDL).

**Live page:** https://marieblanchebotilou.github.io/authors-seat-simulator/

## Why it exists
Reviewers rarely see their comments from the receiving end. This simulation opens the module by putting learners in the author's seat: they receive a "Major Revision" decision letter, read three reviewer comments about an inaccessible figure, and decide how to respond to each. The contrast between a vague comment, a harsh prescriptive one, and a specific, option-giving one sets up the rest of the module, where learners practice writing feedback that is clear, respectful, and grounded in UDL principles.

## Notes
- All manuscript content, names, and reviews are fictional.
- Built as a self-contained HTML page and linked from our Articulate Rise module.
- Developed with AI assistance from our team's design.
